Campaign Overview: Phantom Wake

"We are the ripples of a storm they never saw coming."

Phantom Wake is a persistent, narrative-driven Task Force campaign where you take command of a rogue maritime Private Military Company (PMC). Built by outcasts, defectors, and renegades who lost their flags, your organization operates in the gray zones of international waters. You are not a superpower; you are a business fighting for survival.

Starting with nothing but a single, aging corvette and a shoestring budget, you must accept low-profile, dangerous contracts to keep your crew paid and your ship afloat. As you complete missions, you will earn resources to expand your PMC, eventually transforming Phantom Wake from a ragtag group of mercenaries into a world-class naval strike group capable of altering the balance of global power.
Core Campaign Features

    The Underdog Beginning
    You do not start with nuclear submarines or carrier strike groups. Your initial Task Force is severely limited by budget. You begin with a single corvette, forcing you to rely on stealth, electronic warfare, and careful maneuvering rather than overwhelming firepower. Every missile fired is money burned.

    Contract-Based Progression
    The campaign is structured around mercenary contracts. You will start with mundane but tense operations: escorting rusty civilian tankers through pirate-infested straits, conducting search-and-rescue operations in hostile waters, and performing covert electronic reconnaissance for anonymous clients.

    Dynamic Fleet Expansion
    Success brings funding. Utilizing the Task Force Builder, you will invest your hard-earned mission points into purchasing better hardware. Upgrade from your starting corvette to surplus frigates, acquire decommissioned destroyers, and eventually field modern guided-missile cruisers or silent hunter-killer submarines.

    Persistent Consequences
    Damage and ammunition carry over between contracts. If your only ship takes a torpedo hit to the engineering bay, you will start the next mission crippled. You must carefully weigh the financial and tactical risks of every engagement. Sometimes, breaking contact and retreating is the only way the PMC survives to fight another day.

    Escalating Threat Level
    As Phantom Wake grows in reputation and firepower, so do your enemies. What begins as skirmishes with local pirates and rogue patrol boats will eventually escalate into proxy wars against state-funded navies and rival PMCs sent to eliminate your growing monopoly.

Rules of Engagement (ROE): Flexible. You are mercenaries. You shoot when fired upon, or when the contract pays enough to shoot first.
